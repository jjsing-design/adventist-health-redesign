# Adventist Health Primary Care — location page

Mobile redesign of the Atascadero primary care location page. The live page repeats the clinic name, phone, and address, hides a predictable weekly schedule behind a tap, and never says whether the office takes new patients or which plans it accepts. People scroll through the same facts and leave.

## What changed

The first screen is one contact card:

- Adventist Health Primary Care
- A service of Adventist Health Physicians Network
- 6955 El Camino Real, Suite 200, Atascadero, CA 93422
- Open • Closes at 5 p.m.
- Mon–Fri 8 a.m. – 5 p.m. | Closed weekends
- Call 805-395-3277 and Get directions

Hours are a pattern, not seven identical rows. Holiday closures are the only list, and they stay closed until someone asks.

Under that, in order:

1. **Before you go** — accepting new patients, four sample plans, four clinicians, and book online or call.
2. **Services** — most common visits called out, then the full list of nine.
3. **Visit details** — parking, accessibility, telehealth, what to bring, and how soon you can be seen. Each row answers itself in one line.
4. **Entrance illustration** — after the facts, not before them.
5. **Nearby Adventist Health** — Templeton, Paso Robles, and San Luis Obispo, each with distance and why you would go there instead.

The phone number and the street address appear once.

## Why the card is not sticky forever

Call and directions have to survive a scroll, and the rest of the page has to stay readable. The full card is the top of the page. After it scrolls off, a short bar keeps status, Call, and Directions on screen. That is the whole sticky behavior.

## How to read the prototype

Status is pinned to Wednesday, Oct 7, 2026, 10:30 a.m., so a review always shows the office open and closing at 5 p.m. Choosing a visit time updates a banner on the page. Nothing is sent to a clinic. Insurance plans, provider names, distances, and the building drawing are sample content for the case study.

## Result

Someone opening the page can see whether the clinic is open, when it closes, where it is, and how to call or get directions without scrolling and without opening the hours. New-patient status, insurance, and who works there are on the next card, not missing.
